MySivi · Ad Library Teardown09 Gaps
09 — Competitive gaps

What nobody in this sample is doing

Each gap starts from a count in the evidence grid, not from opinion.

01

Missing Transformation Proof

0 out of 10 ads use a before-and-after visual format

Why it matters. Users cannot visualize the specific skill improvement they are buying

Opportunity. Test a split-screen showing broken English vs. fluent English using the same prompt

02

Lack of Peer Validation

Only 1 out of 10 ads features a real learner story

Why it matters. AI characters can feel uncanny and reduce trust in educational outcomes

Opportunity. Source 15-second selfie testimonials from actual users

03

Slow Product Identification

Median product reveal is 16s; ad 1083327227503975 waits until 55s

Why it matters. Feed users drop off before seeing the app interface

Opportunity. Insert a 2-second UI pop-up within the first 5 seconds of every skit

04

Untapped Regional Markets

Sample lacks Telugu, Bengali, and Marathi creatives

Why it matters. These represent high-volume segments for English learning apps in India

Opportunity. Localize existing winning AI scripts into Telugu and Bengali
